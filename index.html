Target Audience

This site is designed for senior leaders and transformation decision-makers who need a concise view of how a digital transformation should be sequenced, governed, and monitored.

What This Site Provides

Transformation Roadmap

Shows what initiatives should happen first, what can be tested next, and what capabilities are required before scaling.

Governance RACI

Shows who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for recurring transformation decisions.

QBR Dashboard

Shows whether initiatives are progressing toward their targets and identifies decisions that require leadership attention.

Together, these tools help leadership connect what should happen, who makes the decisions, and how progress is measured.

Our Novelty

Our team did not simply summarize NIKE's digital transformation. We converted the published case into an integrated management framework that connects sequence, governance, and performance measurement.

First, we organized NIKE's initiatives into a Crawl/Walk/Run roadmap with explicit dependencies to show how foundational capabilities support later consumer-facing initiatives. Second, we translated the transformation into recurring governance decisions, assigning realistic NIKE leadership roles as Accountable and connecting each decision to the roadmap. Third, we created a QBR dashboard that connects leading and lagging metrics to named initiatives and identifies the leadership decisions required in the current quarter.

Our additional contribution as a team is the integration of these three views into one consistent artifact, with shared initiative names, decision connections, evidence, and cross-deliverable consistency checks.
