Crawl Phase Rationale

The Crawl phase builds three main capabilities that the Walk initiatives depend on, which are direct relationships with consumers, data and analytics, and faster end-to-end operations. These initiatives can be developed mostly at the same time because they do not depend on each other to be completed first. Nike App at Retail needs these capabilities to provide more personalized retail experiences and respond faster to consumer needs. NIKE Hyperlive also depends on consumer information and analytics to make better local retail decisions. NIKE needs to build these capabilities first before testing them with consumers during the Walk phase.

Walk Phase Rationale

The Walk phase takes the capabilities developed during Crawl and tests them in real consumer-facing environments before expanding them further. Nike App at Retail tests how membership, analytics, and faster operations can work together to connect digital and in-store experiences. NIKE Hyperlive tests how consumer information and analytics can help NIKE make better decisions for local stores. Member adoption, conversion, consumer engagement, and assortment performance can show whether these initiatives are working. These results are important before moving to Run and investing in a much larger rollout.

Run (Scale)

Nike Consumer Experiences at Scale

Quarter Range: Q7–Q9

Walk Dependency: Nike App at Retail

Full-scale Deployment Scope: Expand Nike Consumer Experiences beyond limited retail environments across NIKE Direct (NIKE owned digital and retail channels) and strategic marketplace partners.

Organizational Change: Digital, retail, technology, supply chain, and other teams need to work together to provide a more connected consumer experience across physical stores, digital channels, and strategic partners.

Consumer Direct Acceleration

Quarter Range: Q7–Q10

Walk Dependency: Nike App at Retail, NIKE Hyperlive

Full-scale Deployment Scope: Expand consumer-focused digital capabilities across NIKE's broader business and marketplace.

Organizational Change: NIKE needs to organize more around the consumer, continue investing in data, analytics, and its end-to-end technology foundation, and make operating and leadership changes that support the transformation at a larger scale.

Run Phase Rationale

The Run phase expands the capabilities after they have already been tested through the Walk initiatives. Nike Consumer Experiences at Scale takes the connected physical and digital experience tested through Nike App at Retail and expands it across NIKE Direct and strategic marketplace partners. Consumer Direct Acceleration takes the ideas tested through Nike App at Retail and NIKE Hyperlive and applies them across the broader organization. At this stage, NIKE also needs stronger coordination between teams, continued technology investment, and organizational changes to make these capabilities work at a larger scale.

Dependency Annotations

NikePlus Membership → Nike App at Retail: enables NIKE to recognize members and provide more personalized experiences across digital and physical retail.
Data & Analytics Capabilities → Nike App at Retail: enables NIKE to use consumer information to provide more personalized retail experiences.
2X Speed and End-to-End Digital Capabilities → Nike App at Retail: enables NIKE to respond faster to consumer needs and support connected retail experiences.
NikePlus Membership → NIKE Hyperlive: enables NIKE to use consumer information to support local retail decisions.
Data & Analytics Capabilities → NIKE Hyperlive: enables NIKE to use consumer data and analytics to make better decisions about products offered in local stores.
Nike App at Retail → Nike Consumer Experiences at Scale: enables NIKE to expand the connection between digital and physical retail across NIKE Direct (NIKE digital and retail channels) and strategic marketplace partners.
Nike App at Retail → Consumer Direct Acceleration: enables NIKE to use tested connected retail experiences as part of its broader digital transformation.
NIKE Hyperlive → Consumer Direct Acceleration: enables NIKE to take what worked in local data-driven retail and apply it across the broader organization.
